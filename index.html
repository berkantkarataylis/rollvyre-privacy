ROLLVYRE – Privacy Policy

Developer: Joyfamous Games BK-DNT
App: ROLLVYRE (com.joyfamousgames.rollvyre)
Last updated: October 28, 2026

1. Data we collect

ROLLVYRE does not collect your name, email address, phone number, location or contacts. Your game progress (coins, diamonds, keys, items, scores, settings) is stored only on your device and is not sent to our servers.

2. Third-party services

Google AdMob (ads): The game may show rewarded and interstitial ads. AdMob may process data such as the advertising ID, device and usage information under Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Google Play Billing (in-app purchases): Coins, diamonds, keys and packs can be purchased with real money through Google Play. Google alone processes your payment details; we never see them. Refunds and cancellations follow Google Play rules.

Google Play Games Services: If you sign in, your scores appear on the leaderboard and your Google Play Games profile information is processed by Google.

3. Children

ROLLVYRE is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13.

4. Data retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device. You can delete all of it by uninstalling the app or clearing its data. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.

5. Changes

This policy may be updated; the current version is always published on this page.
